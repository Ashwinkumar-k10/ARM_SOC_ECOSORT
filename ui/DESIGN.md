## Brand & Style

The design system projects an authoritative, premium commercial-grade environmental stewardship standard. Crafted for modern facilities management, corporate sustainability directors, and discerning commercial tenants, the visual demeanor balances rigorous institutional utility with crisp architectural luxury.

The aesthetic philosophy is rooted in **Refined Minimalist Functionalism**:
- **Clarity over Clutter**: Rejecting dense industrial HUD gauges, neon metrics, and gratuitous glassmorphic blurs in favor of generous, deliberate whitespace.
- **Architectural Precision**: Structural lines, controlled spatial rhythms, and crisp typographics echo modern sustainable architecture rather than consumer gimmicks.
- **Restrained Semantic Authority**: Waste stream identification relies on absolute clarity, deploying calibrated semantic markers exclusively where critical categorization decisions occur.
- **Progressive Disclosure**: High-level ecological impact and sorting status remain immediately scannable; deep machine-learning telemetry, contamination analysis, and sensor diagnostics reveal themselves purposefully on demand.

## Colors

The palette establishes an executive, commercial tone through high-contrast structural tones anchored by deep forest greens and clinical, sterile surfaces.

### Core Canvas & Structure
- **Primary Canvas**: `#FFFFFF` provides absolute purity, high legibility, and architectural crispness.
- **Secondary Canvas**: `#F7F8F7` serves as structural surface grounding, differentiating panels, modules, and grouped controls without introducing visual weight.
- **Primary Text**: `#101820` (Off-Obsidian) delivers stark, uncompromised legibility while avoiding the harsh optical vibration of pure black.
- **Secondary Text / Neutral**: `#59636E` provides calm, accessible contrast for metadata, secondary indicators, and dimensional metrics.
- **Structural Borders**: `#E4E7E5` defines boundaries with subtle, whisper-thin authority.

### Brand & Interactive Foundation
- **Primary Brand Anchor**: `#0D3B31` (Deep Forest Evergreen) serves as the primary focal anchor, active navigation state, and critical commitment action.
- **Interactive Action Accent**: `#124E40` elevates interactive hover states and secondary active indicators.

### Semantic Waste Categorization (Strictly Segregated)
To prevent cognitive overload and maintain strict compliance standards, categorical hues are barred from decorative backgrounds and restricted entirely to categorical cues, badges, and status pips:
- **Dry Recyclable**: `#2563EB` (Cobalt Core) — Clean paper, cardboard, plastics, metals.
- **Organic Compost**: `#15803D` (Verdant Flora) — Food remnants, compostable packaging, organic fibers.
- **Special-Care / Hazardous**: `#B91C1C` (Crimson Alert) — E-waste, bio-hazards, chemical batteries, regulated items.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy marries structural precision with high-speed executive parsing:
- **Headlines (`Hanken Grotesk`)**: Imparts an architectural, modern confidence. Tight tracking (`-0.02em`) on display headings ensures crisp, editorial distinction without drifting into decorative expressionism.
- **Body & Controls (`Inter`)**: Delivers supreme optical clarity across low-emission screens, sensor panels, and handheld inventory devices. Renders numerical performance data and waste classifications with surgical neutrality.
- **Labels & Categorical Badges**: Tracked out uppercase typography (`letter-spacing: 0.04em - 0.05em`) in medium-to-bold weights establishes immediate distinction between operational state descriptors and dynamic data values.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is anchored to a strict **8px baseline metric system** designed to sustain balance across both spatial facility dashboards and handheld industrial scanners.

### Breakpoints & Canvas Bounds
- **Mobile Handheld (360px - 767px)**: Fluid 4-column framework. Screen margins are locked strictly to 20px–24px (`1.25rem`–`1.5rem`), ensuring elements clear physical device bezels while maximizing central scanning field-of-view.
- **Tablet / Mounted Terminal (768px - 1023px)**: 8-column layout with 24px gutters. Optimized for horizontal facility kiosks with side navigation rail anchoring.
- **Desktop / Control Deck (1024px+)**: 12-column grid capped at 1440px max width to preserve structural line length and prevent data dispersion across ultra-wide monitors.

### Spacing Principles
- **Touch-Target Integrity**: Every interactive hit-box maintains a strict minimum footprint of `48px x 48px` to guarantee zero-latency execution in high-throughput sorting corridors.
- **Information Density Control**: Telemetry widgets preserve `1.5rem` (24px) internal airiness to prevent visual entanglement between disparate stream diagnostics.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids theatrical gradients, drop shadows, and simulated blur layers. Instead, depth is articulated through **tonal differentiation, physical line work, and ambient containment**:

1. **Surface Layering**:
   - Level 0 (Canvas Base): `#FFFFFF`
   - Level 1 (Container Deck): `#F7F8F7` demarcates grouped interactive areas and telemetry trays.
   - Level 2 (Floating Action Overlays / Modal Drawer): `#FFFFFF` elevated solely by a low-amplitude, natural tinted boundary shadow.

2. **Ambient Shadow Matrix**:
   - `shadow-subtle`: `0 1px 2px 0 rgba(16, 24, 32, 0.04)` — applied to resting structural cards.
   - `shadow-focus`: `0 4px 12px -2px rgba(13, 59, 49, 0.08)` — reserved for elevated inspection cards and popover confirmation dialogues, tinted subtly with the primary forest seed.

3. **Structural Partitioning**:
   - Primary separation relies on crisp `1px solid #E4E7E5` borders rather than heavy contrast offsets, anchoring widgets with quiet precision.

## Shapes

The design system implements a disciplined **Roundedness Level 2** architectural profile. Curves are subtle and structural, projecting institutional reliability rather than playful softness.

- **Base Components (Inputs, Small Badges, Action Buttons)**: `0.5rem` (8px). Delivers a modern, softened silhouette that remains structurally grounded.
- **Cards, Panels, and Containers (`rounded-lg`)**: `1rem` (16px). Harmonizes large structural content zones with smooth corner transitions.
- **Overlays, Full-Screen Sheets, and Inspection Modals (`rounded-xl`)**: `1.5rem` (24px). Creates a distinct frame boundary for elevated operational state diagnostics.
- **Pill Badges (`rounded-full`)**: Exceptionally used only for single-metric compliance status indicators and micro classification pips.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Action**: Solid `#0D3B31` fill, `#FFFFFF` text, `0.5rem` border radius, minimum height of `48px`. Hover state smoothly transitions to `#124E40`. Active focus is reinforced with a `2px` offset ring in `#0D3B31`.
- **Secondary Action**: Background transparent, `#E4E7E5` structural border, `#101820` text. Hover transitions to `#F7F8F7`.
- **Destructive / Flag Warning**: Background `#FFFFFF`, border `#B91C1C`, text `#B91C1C`. Never used decoratively; reserved exclusively for emergency halt or contamination override.

### Semantic Category Badges & Stream Chips
- Badges must never rely on color alone; each badge pairs an explicit textual category title with an ISO waste icon.
- Backgrounds use a 10% tint of the categorical color, paired with a solid 1px border and bold categorical text:
  - **Dry Recyclable**: Tinted `#2563EB10` background, `#2563EB` border and text.
  - **Organic Compost**: Tinted `#15803D10` background, `#15803D` border and text.
  - **Special-Care**: Tinted `#B91C1C10` background, `#B91C1C` border and text.

### Inspection Cards & Machine Telemetry Modules
- Base card: Flat `#FFFFFF` surface with a `1px solid #E4E7E5` outline, `1rem` corner rounding, and `1.5rem` internal padding.
- **Telemetry Trays**: Machine-learning diagnostics (confidence scores, optical classification logs, sensor weight readouts) nest within an inner card wrapped in `#F7F8F7`. Technical metrics utilize progressive disclosure accordions, hidden under an understated "Sensor Telemetry" toggle to eliminate executive clutter.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Standard input height is `48px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E4E7E5`, radius `0.5rem`.
- Focus state brings a sharp transition to `1px solid #0D3B31` paired with an ambient green focus ring (`0 0 0 3px rgba(13, 59, 49, 0.12)`).
- Placeholder text utilizes `#59636E` at a 75% opacity for effortless scannability.

### Checkboxes, Radios, & Toggles
- Minimum tap targets are strictly enforced at `48px x 48px` using invisible padding boundaries.
- Unchecked state: `1.5px solid #59636E` on `#FFFFFF`.
- Checked state: `#0D3B31` solid fill with crisp `#FFFFFF` check geometry.

### Stream Sorting List Items
- Lists leverage clean divider lines (`1px solid #E4E7E5`) without alternating zebra striping.
- Left edge features an optional 3px vertical color indicator corresponding strictly to the resolved semantic stream (Dry, Organic, or Special-Care).
